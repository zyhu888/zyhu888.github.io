Hello!

I’m Zeyu Hu. Thank you for stopping by my website!

I’m a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Statistics at the University of Connecticut. My research focuses on statistical methods for complex networks and high-dimensional data, particularly covariate-informed clustering, Bayesian modeling, and MCMC computation, with applications to functional brain networks and genomics. My applied work spans epidemiology and public health, including spatiotemporal and longitudinal studies of infectious disease and aging cohorts, as well as Bayesian design and analysis for pediatric clinical trials.
